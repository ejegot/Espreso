CoffeeSpot — Merchant Account Checklist

GCash for Business + Maya Business (QR Ph payments)

Para makatanggap ang shop ng GCash at Maya payments sa ordering system, kailangan mo munang mag-apply bilang merchant sa GCash at Maya. Sundin ang checklist na ito step by step.

1 Ihanda ang mga dokumento

Isang folder lang — photo o PDF ng bawat isa:

  • DTI Certificate of Business Name Registration
  • BIR Form 2303 (Certificate of Registration)
  • Mayor's Permit / Business Permit (kung meron na)
  • Valid government ID ng owner (may signature)
  • Proof of business address (utility bill o lease)
  • Proof of bank account (passbook o statement) — settlement account
  • Bagong business email — hindi pa nagamit sa GCash o Maya Business

Personal accounts (kailangan din)

  • Personal GCash — fully verified, mas okay kung 1 taon+ na active
  • Personal Maya — verified
Important: Dapat mag-match ang business name sa lahat ng documents (yung registered name sa DTI/BIR).

2 GCash for Business

Website: business.gcash.com
Review time: karaniwang 3–7 business days

  1. Mag-apply sa business.gcash.com
  2. Gamitin ang bagong business email
  3. Business type: Sole Proprietorship (o tama sa documents mo)
  4. I-upload: DTI, BIR 2303, ID, address proof, bank proof
  5. Submit at hintayin ang approval
  6. Pag approved → login sa GCash Business dashboard
  7. Pumunta sa Receive Payments → Generate QR Code
  8. Piliin ang QR Ph (hindi yung GCash-only QR)
  9. I-download ang QR image
  10. Mag-test: magpadala ng ₱1 sa QR gamit ang phone mo
  11. I-print ang isang kopya para backup sa counter
Fees (typical): ~2.5% + ₱3 per transaction — i-check sa dashboard
Settlement: karaniwang next business day sa bank account mo

3 Maya Business

Website: maya.ph/business
App: Maya Business (Google Play Store)
Support: (02) 8845-7700
Review time: ilang araw hanggang ~2 linggo

Full business account

  1. Mag-register sa maya.ph/business o sa Maya Business app
  2. Piliin ang Maya Business Manager (full account)
  3. I-verify ang email at mobile number
  4. I-upload ang same docs (DTI, BIR, ID, bank)
  5. Hintayin ang verification

Pag approved na

  1. Buksan ang Maya Business Manager o app
  2. Hanapin ang Maya QR o QR Ph section
  3. I-download ang merchant QR image
  4. Mag-test: magpadala ng ₱1 sa QR
  5. I-print ang isang kopya para sa counter
Fees (typical): ~1.5% per transaction — i-check sa contract/dashboard
Kung matagal ang full account, pwede magtanong sa Maya support kung may mas mabilis na QR-only option.

5 Habang naghihintay ng approval

  • Pwede pa ring mag-operate ang shop — cash sa counter
  • Pwede ring manual GCash/Maya sa personal account (temporary lang)
  • Pag may QR images na, ilalagay na sa ordering system ng shop
